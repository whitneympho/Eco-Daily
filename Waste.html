Waste

What is Waste

Waste is anything we throw away because we no longer need it or want it. Each day businesses and households produce waste.
When we don't manage waste properly it can lead to pollution and also harm wildlife

Reduce

Buy only what you need and avoid unnecesary packaging to create less waste

Reuse

Give your old items a second life by donating fixing or using them again

Recycle

Recycle items such as paper plastic cans bottles and metal to help protect the enviroment

The impact of waste

Land Pollution

Land pollution happens whwen solid or liquid waste, chemicals or trash accumulate or or under the earths surface damaging soil quality and ecosystems and public health

Ocean Pollution

Ocean pollution refers to the contamination of marine waters by human generated substances like plastic chemical runoff,untreated sewage oil and noise

Climate Change

Climate change refers to long term shifts of temperatures and weather patterns primarily driven by human avtivities and the burning of fossil fuels.
